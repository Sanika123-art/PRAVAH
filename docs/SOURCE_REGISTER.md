# SAKSHAM — Official Source Register
## Statutory & Regulatory Evidence Base for AICTE Approval Workflows

---

### 1. Document Objective
This register catalogues all official statutory documents, procedural handbooks, circulars, corrigenda, and court-mandated procedural guidelines governing AICTE approval workflows, compliance standards, and committee inspections.

In accordance with strict regulatory compliance principles, requirements or workflow rules derived from these documents are flagged with their explicit verification status. **Unverified or pending provisions are clearly noted as such.**

### 3. Verification Protocol & Treatment of Rules
1. **Rule Versioning:** All workflow parameters (SLA days, required documents, committee sizes) are tied to a specific `Source ID` and `Effective Period`.
2. **Flagging Unverified Policies:** Any business rule or transition interval that is proposed or inferred without explicit handbook reference is flagged as `PENDING_VERIFICATION` in the regulatory rules register.
3. **No Autonomous Approval:** AICTE Act Section 10(k) mandates that approvals may only be granted by the statutory bodies. SAKSHAM enforces this by maintaining an administrative, audit-logged workflow rather than automated sign-off.
