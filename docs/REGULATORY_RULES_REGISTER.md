### 3. Configurable Parameter Defaults (Subject to Admin Overrides)
- **Scrutiny Turnaround SLA:** 7 business days from date of assignment.
- **EVC Inspection Window:** Maximum 14 calendar days from committee notification.
- **EVC Report Submission Window:** 48 hours post-visit.
- **Institutional Rectification Period (Re-Scrutiny):** 10 calendar days.
- **Document Expiry Early Warning Window:** 180 days (Warning), 90 days (Urgent), 30 days (Critical Action).
- **Compliance Undertaking Grace Period:** 90 calendar days from conditional approval grant.
